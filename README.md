# OTT Subscription Churn & Revenue Analytics


## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing OTT (Over-The-Top) subscription data to understand customer churn patterns, subscription behavior, and revenue performance.

By integrating subscriber, subscription, and customer information across multiple relational tables using SQL and Python, the project identifies customer segments at risk of churning and uncovers opportunities to improve customer retention and recurring revenue.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns and subscription behavior.
- Evaluate revenue performance and recurring revenue trends.
- Identify high-risk customer segments based on tenure, contract type, and subscription plans.
- Understand the factors contributing to subscriber loss.
- Develop data-driven strategies to improve customer retention and revenue growth.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Python | Data analysis and processing |
| SQL | Data extraction and relational data integration |
| Pandas | Data cleaning, manipulation, and analysis |
| Jupyter Notebook | Analysis and documentation |
| Data Visualization | Presenting trends and analytical findings |

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Analyzed 20+ business metrics, including:

- **Customer Churn Rate:** Percentage of subscribers who discontinue their subscriptions.
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Recurring revenue generated from active subscriptions.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV):** Estimated long-term revenue contribution of a customer.
- **Subscription Trends:** Changes in subscription patterns over time.
- **Customer Retention:** Measurement of customers continuing their subscriptions.
- **Revenue Analysis:** Evaluation of revenue distribution across customer segments.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

1. **Data Integration:** Combined subscriber, subscription, and customer data using SQL and Pandas.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, inconsistent records, and prepared datasets for analysis.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined customer behavior, subscription patterns, and revenue trends.
4. **Churn Analysis:** Evaluated churn across contract types, subscription tenure, and plan tiers.
5. **Revenue Analysis:** Investigated MRR, CLTV, and revenue distribution to identify potential revenue leakage.
6. **Data Visualization:** Created charts and summary reports to communicate important findings.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to migrate towards longer-term subscription contracts.
- Identify high-risk customer segments and introduce targeted retention campaigns.
- Improve customer engagement through personalized subscription offers.
- Monitor churn patterns and recurring revenue to support better business decisions.


## 📈 Project Outcome

Developed an end-to-end churn and revenue analytics workflow that transforms raw subscription data into actionable business insights. The analysis helps identify high-risk customer segments, understand revenue patterns, and formulate data-driven retention strategies.
